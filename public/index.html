Click the floor (or use WASD / arrow keys) to move. Press Enter to chat. Press F or double-tap to throw fire (2s cooldown) — land two hits on someone and they respawn at the center. Find the chest at the bottom of the lounge for a free random hat, and the glowing chest on the left for a temporary attack power-up — it unlocks every 10 fire hits on the leaderboard. Walk through the doorway on the right to enter the boss chamber: dodge the big slime's fireballs (two hits sends you back to the lounge), land 30 hits on it to defeat it, and grab the magical sword resting behind it — or steal it from whoever's currently holding it.
